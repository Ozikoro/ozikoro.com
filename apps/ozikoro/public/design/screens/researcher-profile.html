Research interests

Precolonial market systems Oral historiography Igbo ritual office Archives and repatriation Niger delta trade

Working on the relationship between the four-day market week and the distribution of ritual office in the northern Igbo towns, using testimony recorded with town councils alongside administrative returns. Current project is a complete transcription programme for recordings deposited with Ozikoro between 2024 and 2026.
