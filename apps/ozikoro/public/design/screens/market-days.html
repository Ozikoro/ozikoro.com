Choose the community calendar first

Igbo communities share the four-day cycle, but do not all use one universal starting point. Ozikoro should calculate a market day only from a named, verified community calendar.

Why Ozikoro asks for a community

Eke, Orie/Oye, Afọ/Afor and Nkwọ/Nkwor repeat every four days, while the date used to anchor that cycle can differ by community. This avoids presenting one local convention as universal.
