How to cite Ozikoro

Choose the kind of record you used. Keep its author or holder, title, version date, permanent link and access date together.

ArticleArchive recordPhotographAudioPublication
Example format

Ozikoro article

Author. “Article title.” Ozikoro, Ozi Ikoro Limited, published date, last updated date. Permanent URL.

Replace this example with the citation shown on the article itself.
Example format

Archive record

Creator or depositing community. “Record title.” Date. Ozikoro reference number, holding institution. Access terms. Permanent URL.

Example format

Photograph

Photographer, if known. “Image description.” Date, place. Collection/holder, Ozikoro reference number. Reuse terms.

Example format

Oral recording

Speaker. Interview by recorder, place, recording date. Ozikoro reference number. Timestamp if relevant. Consent/access terms.

Example format

Research publication

Use the publication’s authors, year, title, journal or collection, version, DOI or permanent Ozikoro URL.
