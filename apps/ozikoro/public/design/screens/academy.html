One institution, three roles

The dictionary gives the word. The archive gives the record the word belongs to. The Academy teaches you to read both. A term in a history links to its dictionary entry; a dictionary entry links to the recording it was taken from; a course session cites the archive entry it is taught from.

They are recognisably different tools and they are plainly the same institution — same type, same spacing, same way of naming a source.
