What this archive holds, and on whose terms

Material deposited by a town, a clan or a family remains theirs. Ozikoro holds it, describes it and makes it findable; the depositor sets whether it is open, available by request, or restricted to the institutional tier. Some material is culturally sensitive and is not published in full.

Requesting material that is not published

Objects marked by request or institutional tier are described in full here but not reproduced. A request goes to the holder — a family, a town union, or the institution that deposited it — and the terms are theirs to set, not ours.
