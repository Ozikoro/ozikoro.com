Two things the implementer must know

  1. Every name, town, count and entry in these screens is example material and is labelled on the page. Nothing here is a record held by Ozikoro, and none of it should ship.
  2. The typeface choice is a hard requirement, not a preference: Noto Serif and Noto Sans carry ị ọ ụ ñ and the combining tone marks in every weight and in italic, so no dotted vowel falls back to a second font. See the typeface proof and NOTES.md before substituting anything.
