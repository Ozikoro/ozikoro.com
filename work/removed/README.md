# Things taken out of the registry, and why

## proverbs-without-english.jsonl

1,848 proverbs removed on the owner's instruction, 2026-09-29:

> "all the proverbs without English translation should be removed. every single one. the
> proverbs that should be there should the ones with english translation already only"

They were every proverb in the database with no English: 1,634 published and 214 drafts. What
is left is **700, and every one of them carries a translation a publication printed**. No
proverb on ozituma.com now has an empty meaning.

Kept here rather than deleted outright for one reason: the Igbo text is not recoverable from
anything else if it is wanted later. Each line carries the proverb, its source and status,
and the dictionary words it was linked to — so a proverb that gets a proper translation later
can be put back with its links intact.

WHAT WENT WITH THEM

12,563 links from dictionary words to these proverbs. 354 words were illustrated only by
proverbs in this file, and 5,023 published words now carry no example of any kind. That is
the cost of the removal and it is recorded here rather than discovered later.
