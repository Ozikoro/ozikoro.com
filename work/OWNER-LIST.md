### Umunri — 6 sentences
https://ozituma.com/clans/umunri

- Umunri is the name the survey enters, and it prints that name jointly with Nri, its leading town.
- Its seniority is contested: the note to Agugu Agu reports that Oreri claims to have founded Nri and to rank above it, a claim Nri denies.
- The survey places the group in the western uplands, the earliest cultural centre of the Northern Igbo, where the graded Ọzọ title system was elaborated before spreading southward to the Agulu and Isuofia areas.
- Afigbo treats Nri as the name of a clan, not of one village, and sets out the tradition that Eri's eldest son Nri had four sons who founded Agukwu, Enugu-Ukwu, Nawfia and Enugu-Agidi — the group he calls the secondary segments, or Umunri.
- Afigbo also lists the services that carried Nri's name across Igboland: removing offences against the earth, installing the eze at Aguleri, tying the ankle cords at Ọzọ title-taking, and preparing the yam medicine for the year.
- Afigbo argues that colonial officers first attached the name Nri to the single town of Agukwu and that Forde & Jones then used Umunri and Nri interchangeably for a four-community clan; he holds that the clan, not the village, bears the name.
