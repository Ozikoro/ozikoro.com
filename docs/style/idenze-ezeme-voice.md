# The Ozikoro house voice — Idenze Ezeme

Extracted from `docs/articles/igbo-izugbe.md`, which is the reference sample. **This is a description of how
the prose behaves, not a set of rules to apply mechanically.** Read the sample before writing; this file is a
map to it, not a substitute for it.

Register: **library and university press.** Never a blog, never a listicle, never hyped.

---

## Structure

**The opening is a concrete observation or a small scene, never a general statement.** The sample opens:
*"Open the entry for **ike** on Ozituma and the page performs a small act of choice before the reader has read
a word."* A lesser version of the same piece would have opened "Dictionaries of Igbo face a difficult
decision." **Start inside the thing, not above it.**

**Section headings are plain noun phrases** and read as a sequence of steps in an argument:
*"Igbo is not one speech" · "What izugbe means" · "Why the headword has to be one variety" · "What happens to
the others" · "Where the choice shows" · "What a standard cannot do".* No gerunds-as-headings ("Understanding
X"), no questions, no colons in every heading.

**The piece takes a position.** It names a problem, argues it, and resolves it. The sample is *for* recording
the varieties and *against* silently discarding them, and it says so.

**The close is aphoristic and reframes the subject** rather than summarising: *"The varieties are not the
standard's errors; they are the language's range, and the range is the older thing."*

## Sentences

**Short declaratives, sometimes standing alone as a paragraph** — *"That page is the subject of this piece,
because the choice it makes is the choice every written dictionary of Igbo has to make, and it is not a
neutral one."*

**The pivot construction "It is not X. It is Y."** — *"That is a description of the standard's authority. It
is not a description of the language."* This is the voice's signature move and it appears more than once.

**Bold lead-ins for the terms of an enumerated argument:** **Search.** **Order.** **Tone.** **Script.** Each
then gets a short paragraph.

**Em-dashes for asides; italics for foreign and technical terms** — *izugbe*, *ike*, *ume*.

## Evidence

**Numbers are precise and specific, or they are absent.** *forty-eight varieties · 12,047 of its 12,232 Igbo
entries · 46,189 recordings · 1,880 proverbs.* **Never a loose round number, and never a number that cannot
be pointed at.** A figure with no source is worse than no figure.

**Concrete examples over abstraction** — the seven varieties under *ike*, *ulo* finding *ụlọ*.

**Uncertainty is shown, not smoothed.** Where a recorded line is fragmentary the sample says so, and says
what is done about it: *"A reading that cannot be established is not published with a warning attached,
because a warning does not stop a reader from taking the reading."*

## Stance

**No colonial framing.** African subjects are treated on their own terms, never as curiosities, never as
awaiting European explanation, never in the passive voice of arrival (*"when the Europeans came"*).

**Oral tradition has equal standing with the written record.** Testimony is a source, not an anecdote.

**Institutions are described by what they do**, not by analogy to European ones.

**No "I".** Third person, institutional, but with a point of view. No hype, no exclamation marks, no
"in conclusion", no "delve", no "it is worth noting".

---

## What this is not

**It is not licence to paraphrase.** Rewriting means: read the source, understand the subject, **search for
more information and verify it**, think about what is actually true and what matters, and then write.
It does not mean substituting synonyms in someone else's sentences.

**The difference is not stylistic, it is evidential.** On nzeora.com, machine substitution turned *notables*
into *"nonentities"* — inverting Azikiwe's role — and *transatlantic* into *"transoceanic"*. Twenty-one of
222 posts there carry that class of corruption. **A synonym spinner produces confident prose about things
that are not true. This voice is the opposite of that**: it is precise because it is grounded.
