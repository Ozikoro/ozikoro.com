This page shows how an Ozikoro story can be read like a chapter in a book. The original story text is not reproduced in this design demonstration.

The telling

The reading measure remains generous, the page stays quiet, and the story’s image appears once—at the opening—so the words can carry the experience.

A verified passage from the storyteller can be given room here.

Demonstration placement only

Short sections, visible place markers and an optional narration keep a long account approachable without turning it into a feed of cards.
