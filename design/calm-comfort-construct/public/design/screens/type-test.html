Typeface proof

Hard constraint 1

The typeface must carry Igbo properly

Noto Serif (headings, long-form) and Noto Sans (interface) both cover Latin Extended Additional and the combining marks. Nothing below falls back to a second font, and no mark collides with the line above at the body leading of 1.72.

Tight leading, stacked marks

Headings run at 1.15. Marks on the lower line must not touch descenders above.

Monospace — reference numbers and citations

If this typeface is ever substituted

Open this page in the replacement and check four things: that no glyph on it renders in a different face; that the dot below and the mark above appear together on ị̀ ọ́ ụ̀; that nothing is clipped at 12px; and that the tight-leading block above has no collision. A face that fails any of the four is disqualified.
