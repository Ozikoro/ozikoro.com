3 · Terms and access

Who may read it

If your work draws on testimony

Recorded testimony belongs to the people who gave it. Confirm that you hold consent for the use you are publishing, and name the terms they set. Where consent covers the transcript but not the audio, say so — the record will show it.

Depositing archive material instead?

Documents, photographs, maps and recordings go through a different route, because the question of who holds them and who may read them has to be settled first — with the family, the town union or the institution, not with us.
