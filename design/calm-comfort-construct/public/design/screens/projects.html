How a project runs

From community request to public record

  1. Listen

    A community, scholar or family proposes a subject.

  2. Research

    Fieldwork, interviews and sources gathered with consent.

  3. Review

    Editors and expert reviewers verify evidence.

  4. Publish

    Records go live with citations and access terms.
