# Complete the Ozikoro experience

## Goal
Turn the current partial showcase into a coherent, premium African history and research platform. The work will cover the public institution and reading experience, support pathways, and every dashboard role named in the master blueprint. All screens remain responsive plain HTML/CSS prototypes for later connection to live data.

## Public experience

### About Ozi Ikoro Limited
- Replace the long text-first page with an image-led institutional introduction, visual mission and principles, platform family, editorial method, milestones, and clear partnership/support pathways.
- Add a people section for founders, writers, authors, and team members using only names, roles, portraits, and biographies verifiably published by Ozikoro. Missing roles will appear as honest vacancies rather than invented people.
- Keep detailed terms, privacy, licensing, and deposit rules accessible without letting legal copy dominate the page.

### Donations, sponsors, and investors
- Create separate Donation, Sponsors, and Investors pages with distinct purposes and navigation.
- Donation: selectable giving amounts/frequency, fund allocation, donor details, anonymous option, review, receipt/thank-you, validation, and unavailable/error states.
- Sponsors: opportunity areas, transparent sponsorship packages, recognition treatment, eligibility/ethics safeguards, sponsor showcase, and enquiry flow.
- Investors: company vision, products, market opportunity, impact and sustainability model, governance/diligence materials, risk-aware language, and an investment enquiry flow.
- These will be complete interactive design demonstrations only; no real payment or message submission will be implied.

### Folklores as a storybook
- Recompose the page as a refined illustrated African storybook: cover-like opening, chapter index, large story spreads, ornamental but restrained page details, and clear next/previous reading paths.
- Use verified Ozikoro story titles and imagery only, with category, reading length, audio/translation availability placeholders only when clearly labelled.
- Make mobile feel like a readable pocket storybook, not a compressed desktop grid.

### Article reading
- Replace the plain sample article with an image-led modern reading template showing headline, authors, contributors, verification/version details, topic and place context, reading progress, chapter navigation, pull quotes, media, footnotes, references, related entities, citation tools, save/share controls, and related reading.
- Add a compact mobile reading toolbar and collapsible contents/references so younger readers can move through long pieces without losing context.
- Use verified live Ozikoro material where available; any structural filler remains visibly labelled as demonstration content.

## Account and work dashboards
- Build one shared dashboard shell with a role switcher for design review, clear navigation, alerts, search, profile/account controls, and responsive mobile navigation.
- Give every blueprint role a complete home view:
  - Reader: saved items, followed topics, history, collections.
  - Student: projects, publications, supervisor/institution, notes, submissions, learning.
  - Teacher: resources, courses, classes/projects, sources, publications.
  - Researcher: publications, projects, datasets, fieldwork, questions, groups, collaborators, citations.
  - Independent Researcher: publications, projects, fieldwork, sources, verification, collaborations.
  - Community Knowledge Holder: community profile, oral traditions, media, submissions, review status.
  - Editor: content queue, entity linking, verification, revisions, tasks.
  - Expert Reviewer: assigned manuscripts, evidence review, decisions, reviewer profile.
  - Admin: system overview, users, content, research, entities, media, sources, moderation, analytics, settings, and audit logs.
- Add supporting screens for profile/account settings, publishing workflow, review workspace, moderation queue, user management, and content detail where the dashboard homes need a destination.

## States and accessibility
- Demonstrate populated, empty, loading, error, permission-denied, and pending-review states across public forms and dashboard work.
- Preserve visible focus, semantic structure, reduced-motion behavior, readable contrast, keyboard access, and first-class 375px layouts.
- Keep the approved black, emerald, gold, bronze, and cream identity, Noto type families, and strict no-invention/no-colonial-framing rules.

## Delivery sequence
1. Build the shared visual/navigation foundations and richer About page.
2. Build Donation, Sponsors, and Investors flows.
3. Rebuild Folklores and the article reader.
4. Build the shared dashboard shell and nine role dashboards.
5. Build publishing, review, moderation, account, and required state screens.
6. Add every screen to the walkthrough, document behavior, and verify desktop/mobile navigation, imagery, overflow, keyboard use, and errors.

## Technical details
- Deliverables stay under the existing static design showcase as plain HTML and CSS; no framework or build step is required to read them.
- Shared patterns will be reusable CSS components rather than page-specific visual duplication.
- External Ozikoro imagery will be used only when verified live and permitted by the existing project brief; no stock portraits or fabricated profiles.
- Forms will validate in the prototype, but payments, enquiries, authentication, persistence, and dashboard data remain clearly non-live for the later backend integration stage.
