A presently unresolved problem is the status of labialised consonants. Ekpeye has non-phonemic
labialisation before back vowels and Williamson and Ohiri-Aniche record this as they considered it
possibly significant for reconstruction. Clark omits and this sometimes creates two transcriptions of the
same word

2.1 Vowels

These can be arranged as follows using the Nigerian orthographic convention of subdots;
+ATR
-ATR
Nearly all Ekpeye words form harmony sets, the vowels will always be either + or - expanded
throughout the word. Exceptions are usually transparent compounds and there is every reason to believe
exceptions are former compounds. Nonetheless, there are some clear exceptions such as;
ishudò
gecko
okpulugwe python
ishâni
bedroom
ugbanuko
